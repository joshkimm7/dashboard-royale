# The Gilded Ace

We recommend canceling the blanket replacement of the ten lowest-earning slot cabinets at a fictional Las Vegas casino. Nine sat in quiet zones, and two had fewer than 60 days on the floor. At the default screen, none showed weak demand against comparable local games. M-1158 was the only payout flag. It earned $36,165 less than its configured payout implied, fell below expectation in 23 of 27 weeks, and had a larger shortfall per wager than all 47 comparable same-game cabinets. That evidence warrants an inspection.

Four historical relocations showed why destination results alone can mislead. Every receiving bank gained relative to its comparison, while only two source–destination pairs improved overall. Adjusted wagers fell 5.8% from April to June as city visitors per day fell 7.2%. The shared decline is consistent with market softness. Falling downtime exposure cannot explain the adjusted January–June decline.

For routine staffing, we trained a weekday forecast on January–April and scored it on 61 May–June days. Mean absolute error was $68,699 versus $82,070 for last week and $218,258 for a frozen constant. The paired interval for the lead over last week crossed zero. At the $1.50 million coverage trigger, both weekday and last-week rules caught 18 of 19 peaks. Weekday averages called for one fewer coverage day in the replay. Check announced events separately.

Fund one $1,200 inspection and retain $98,800. Full correction would recover about $5,794 over the July planning window under historical play. The fix probability is unknown. Verify the fault, price any repair, and compare four post-repair weeks with matched peers.

Defer the $1,800 relocation. At 50% capture, the scenario earns $196 before displaced play and loses $6 at 10% displacement. If authorized, measure both affected banks against comparison banks.

Hold a $15,000 promotion weekend for a qualified pilot. At catalog cost, it needs 25.3% of the weaker historical lift to break even. Historical costs and longer-run substitution are uncertain. Require registration and all-in cost evidence, then compare floor-wide contribution through seven days after the event.
