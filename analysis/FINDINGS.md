# Hidden-pattern hunt (P10)

Reproduce with `python3 analysis/hidden_patterns.py <official-data>` (about 5 seconds; prints every number below).
Each candidate was tested against an alternative explanation and a placebo. Only results that survived and that change a decision or the manager's understanding reached the dashboard. `build_data.py` re-derives those two for the app, and `npm run check` ties the quoted figures to it.

| # | Candidate | Tables | Alternative / placebo | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | One cabinet pays on a different share of spins than its published game math | `machine_day`, `game_math`, `sessions` | Lucky players: carded and uncarded rates match. Bet mix: same rate at every bet. Recent fault: high from week 1. Chance: the other 309 cabinets have z mean +0.12, SD 1.01 and max \|z\| 2.80, which is what luck alone predicts. | **Survives, shown.** M-1158 paid on 38.6% of spins against a published 31.5% (z ≈ 124), in 27 of 27 weeks. It gives the existing hold-gap flag a mechanism (the paytable behaves differently) and a verification metric: one day of play (about 3,500 spins, gap ≈ 9 daily SEs) confirms a fix, where hold needs four weeks. Audit → Adjusted ranking → *Hit-frequency check*. |
| C2 | The May 1 comp repricing brought comp users back more often | `trips`, `loyalty_ledger` | Difference in differences against other recent guests, with placebo splits at Mar 6, Apr 3 and May 29. | **Robust null, shown.** The difference is +2.0% (95% −3.1% to +7.0%), inside the placebo range of +0.5% to +4.5%. The cheaper comps raised comp cost per expected win and bought no visible visits. Guests → Comps → *Did cheaper comps bring guests back?* |
| C3 | Comps displace cash food-and-drink sales, a hidden cost of the repricing | `fnb_transactions`, `trips` | Per carded visit, in 28-day windows before and after May 1. Uncarded tickets serve as a control. | **Rejected.** Cash tickets per 100 visits held at 25.7 to 26.6 through the repricing, and comp tickets rose only from 17.0 to 17.6. Cash per visit dipped only in the last window, when uncarded tickets fell more (449 to 377 a day). That points to lower traffic, not substitution. Comp cost is counted only once, from the ledger. |
| C4 | Tampering-attempt reports give early warning of machine faults | `security_incidents`, `operations` | 7 days after a report against the 7 days before it, and against 200 shuffled-date placebos. | **Rejected.** There were 0.20 faults per report in both the 7 days after and the 7 days before. The placebo mean was 0.19 (95% 0.11 to 0.28). No machine-linked report touched M-1158. Reports remain leads, never accusations. |
| C5 | Security workload is mismatched to staffing | `security_incidents`, `shift_roster`, `zone_traffic_hourly` | Rate per 1,000 entries (offline counter hours excluded). Stability between Jan–Mar and Apr–Jun. | **Real but not surfaced.** Staffing is identical in every zone (1 / 2 / 1 officers by shift, never short). The bar has 2.3 reports per 1,000 entries on swing and grave, against 1.2 to 1.5 in most zones, and the pattern is stable (rank ρ = 0.99). Security staffing is outside the July slot budget, and entries undercount seated bar guests, so the rate is uncertain. It is noted as an operations lead only. |
| C6 | The eight cabinets installed together on Mar 24 show a novelty effect that fades | `placements`, `operations`, `prepared_daily_view` | Expected win per available hour against the other Pit cabinets, by fortnight since install. | **Rejected.** The ratio stays at 1.40 to 1.46 for 14 weeks, with no decay. The placements history holds only 4 moves and no configuration changes, both already used by the move and payout-config checks. |
| C7 | A shift in guest mix (locals, conventions) explains the April→June decline | `visitor_profile_monthly` | April vs June estimates against their published sampling SEs. | **Rejected.** Every metric has \|z\| < 1.5: locals 49.3% to 55.0% (z 1.39), conventions 8.3% to 5.3% (z −1.46), visit minutes 71.5 to 70.7. Survey loyalty-card use (67.6%) matches the carded share of spins (68.1%), which supports the carded reconciliation. |
| C8 | Guests return less often, or stay for shorter visits, as demand eases | `trips`, `sessions` | Month-to-month return rate and median carded visit length. | **Rejected.** The return rate stays between 25.1% and 27.5% and the median visit between 48.9 and 49.6 minutes, with no trend. |

Privacy: every result is aggregated (cabinets, zones, cohorts of thousands of guests). No single guest is reported.
